- Omitted: Prep p61-65 gives spare receptors = KD50 - EC50. Not stated in KDT8 ch4 or GG14 ch3; spare-receptor card uses the definition only.
- Not used: Prep p51-55 calls irreversible competitive blockers uncompetitive antagonists. KDT8 ch4 classes nonequilibrium block as a type of noncompetitive antagonism; cards follow KDT8 ch4.
- Not used: Prep p61-65 line reading fastest receptor under 1 msec beside the GPCR heading. KDT8 ch4 gives milliseconds for ion channels and seconds for GPCRs; cards follow KDT8 ch4.
- Not used: Prep uses Km for potency. Km is an enzyme term; potency cards use EC50 per KDT8 ch4.
- Not used: RR5 pp1-5 has garbled sentences (e.g. Gc splitting GTP; dose inversely proportional to potency). Omitted.
- Ambiguous: KDT8 ch4 Table 4.1 text places AT1 and EP3 in more than one column. AT1 left out of all G-protein lists; EP3 kept under Gi only.
- Not used: study deck p1-pd-014 lists tachyphylaxis to ephedrine, tyramine, nicotine. Not found in KDT8 ch4 or the review books checked; no card.
