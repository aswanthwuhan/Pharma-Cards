## Corrections and deletions

- rf-p1-intro-routes-0062: corrected spelling: chapter text prints 'altaplase'; written as alteplase.
- rf-p1-intro-routes-0194: corrected: chapter PDF prints '1-5 mm'; KDT8 8e full text prints micrometres (1-5 um), the only physically sensible unit.
- rf-p1-intro-routes-0196: corrected: chapter PDF prints '<1 mm'; KDT8 8e full text prints micrometres.
- No card deleted.

## Review-book points deliberately not used

- RR5 p1-5: 'Which route has 100% bioavailability? Inhalation route (IV)' is garbled; its statement that first-pass occurs only in oral and rectal routes conflicts with KDT8 ch1 (lungs give a minor first-pass). Not used.
- Prep p16-20 inhalation sizes (local 2-5 um, systemic under 2 um) differ from KDT8 ch1 (1-5 um reaches bronchioles, under 1 um exhaled). KDT8 wins; not used.
- Prep p16-20 high-oral-dose list includes GTN, which KDT8 ch1 says is destroyed in the liver after oral dosing. Not used.
- Sarkar p36-40 EMA orphan threshold ('up to 10,000 EU people') is garbled in OCR. Not used.
