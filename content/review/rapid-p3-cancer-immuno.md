# Review: p3-cancer-immuno (Rapid Fire)

Grounding pass under GROUNDING-RULES.md. Sources: KDT8 ch64-65 (topic refs), GG14 ch39, ch42, ch70, ch71, ch72, RR5 p46-50, Sarkar p476-480. Cards: 362 (class: 28). ok: 350, corrected: 12, deleted: 0.

## Corrections (newer or owner-book text used over KDT8)
- Allopurinol with azathioprine or oral 6-MP: KDT8 dose value is not legible in the text extract. Filled from GG14 ch42 (dose 25% to 33% of usual). GG14 ch70 gives a 75% cut for oral 6-MP. GG14 ch39 says avoid the azathioprine plus allopurinol combination. Both points kept.
- Doxorubicin cardiomyopathy: KDT8 gives over 300 mg/m2. GG14 ch70 gives 550 mg/m2 total (about 20% risk) and advises 300 mg/m2 for children. GG14 used.
- Epirubicin dose: KDT8 gives 60 to 90 mg/m2. GG14 ch70 gives 100 to 120 mg/m2 every 3 to 4 weeks. GG14 used.
- Daclizumab: KDT8 gives a 3-week half-life and still lists it for transplant. GG14 ch39 gives 20 days and says it is now produced only for multiple sclerosis. GG14 used.
- Muromonab-CD3: KDT8 ch65 says it is still occasionally used, and RR5 p46-50 lists it for organ rejection. GG14 ch39 says it is no longer marketed. GG14 used.
- Trastuzumab cardiac risk: KDT8 gives about 1% CHF. GG14 ch72 gives under 5% LVEF fall, under 1% symptomatic heart failure, and about 20% with doxorubicin. GG14 used.
- Leucovorin rescue timing after high-dose methotrexate: GG14 ch70 says within 24 h of the infusion. GG14 used.

## Study-deck claims NOT carried over (not found in the books checked)
- Study deck p3-cancer-immuno-017: leucovorin continued until methotrexate falls at 48 h. Not in GG14 ch70 or KDT8 ch64 text; omitted. The "normal cells rescued more than tumour cells" claim is worded in KDT8 as "professed"; omitted.
- Study deck p3-cancer-immuno-032: rituximab hepatitis B and JC virus reactivation (cited GG14 ch72). Not found in GG14 ch72 text checked; omitted.
- Study deck p3-cancer-immuno-048: TNF-inhibitor tuberculosis risk (cited KDT8 ch15). Not in KDT8 ch65 or GG14 ch39 text checked; omitted.
- Study deck p3-cancer-immuno-043 cites KDT8 for the allopurinol cut (one-quarter to one-third); the Rapid card cites GG14 ch42 for the same cut.
