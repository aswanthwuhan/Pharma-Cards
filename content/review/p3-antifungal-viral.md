## Main corrections
- 049: abacavir hypersensitivity linked to HLA-B*5701; test before starting (Katzung16 ch49, GG14 ch64).
- 053, 057, 059: dolutegravir now the usual starting agent, often as generic TDF + lamivudine + dolutegravir (GG14 ch64); efavirenz an alternative in pregnancy (Katzung16 ch49). The NACO "TLD" policy itself is not printed in the books.
- 061: TAF/FTC and long-acting cabotegravir PrEP (GG14 ch64). 062: dolutegravir used for PEP (Katzung16 ch49); garbled paediatric PEP point removed. 064: agents recommended in pregnancy (Katzung16 ch49).
- 043, 044: simeprevir replaced by the GG14 list of DAAs; SVR12, pangenotypic regimens. 042: no renal restriction on sofosbuvir (GG14 ch63).
- 012: oral ketoconazole hepatotoxicity and adrenal insufficiency (GG14 ch61). 006, 011: isavuconazole for mucormycosis. 007: fluconazole + flucytosine induction (GG14 ch61).
- Added 066 isavuconazole, 067 baloxavir, 068 letermovir (GG14 ch61, ch62).
