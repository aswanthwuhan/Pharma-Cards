## Main corrections
- 045 (flagged): rewritten to compare NICE 2011 and JNC 8 (KDT8 ch41) with ACC/AHA 2017 (130/80 threshold and target) and ESC 2018 (140/90, systolic target <140 close to 130), with SPRINT, all from GG14 ch32.
- 057 (flagged): aspirin in pre-eclampsia: KDT 80 to 100 mg from week 12 to delivery, plus GG14 ch42 (81 or 100 mg started early, reduces preterm pre-eclampsia, evening dosing). Newer 150 mg regimens are not printed in the books, so not added.
- 029: tadalafil gap: "about 48 hours" is not printed in the books; card now says a longer gap than 24 h is needed after tadalafil (GG14 ch31).
- 013: IV magnesium sulfate 1 to 2 g for torsades now sourced from GG14 ch34.
- 007, 017: amiodarone mortality claim qualified (benefit in HF not confirmed in SCD-HeFT, GG14 ch34).
- 030, 038: beta blockers are no longer called the only antianginal class prolonging survival; mortality benefit is after MI and in HF (Katzung16 ch12).
- 032, 033, 036: trimetazidine EMA second-line restriction and movement disorders; nicorandil contraindicated with PDE-5 inhibitors; ivabradine phosphenes and no added benefit in angina (GG14 ch31).
- 058, 060: moved to the antihypertensive-classes subtopic; ESC 2018 single-pill combination and resistant-hypertension step added; NICE scheme labelled as the 2011 version printed in KDT.
