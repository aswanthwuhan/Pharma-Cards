Sources: KD Tripathi 8e ch56 (antitubercular) and ch57 (antileprotic); Goodman & Gilman 14e ch65 (TB and leprosy) and ch64 (HIV ART, for dolutegravir); review-book OCR where cited (Sarkar, Rapid Revision 5e). The validated study deck (content/decks/p3-tb-leprosy.json) was used as a cross-check for GG14 points, and each GG14 point was checked against the GG14 text.

## Corrections and omissions (not web-checked; no owner book contradicted a card)
- Clofazimine WHO group: GG14 Table 65-6 and the study deck place it in Group B. GG14 text (Clofazimine section) also says Group A. Card follows the table (Group B). Not resolved in the books; flagged here.
- Clofazimine half-life: KDT8 says about 70 days; GG14 says about 35 days after sustained repeated dosing. Both are cited on one card.
- Study deck claim "injectables largely phased out" (GG14): not found in the GG14 text for ch65. Omitted.
- Study deck Group A/B/C wording was checked against GG14 Table 65-6; PAS listed in Group C, as in the text.
- Study deck dolutegravir card (GG14 ch64: 50 mg once daily to twice daily with rifampicin; not with nevirapine): verified in GG14 text and kept.
- Study deck claim on efflux-based cross-resistance: GG14 names pepQ mutations (efflux) giving clofazimine and bedaquiline cross-resistance; card worded accordingly.
- Study deck Group A linezolid dose: not used. KDT gives 600 mg for XDR RNTCP regimen (kept); study deck "lactic acidosis" not found in the extracted text (omitted).
- Pyrazinamide dose: KDT gives 25 mg/kg (20 to 30); GG14 gives 15 to 30 mg/kg. KDT figure used; GG14 range not added.
- Ethionamide dosing: KDT (250 mg/day rising to 750 mg/day) and GG14 (250 mg twice daily rising to 1 g/day) differ. No dosing card written; only ADR and role cards kept.
- Sarkar p561-565 pretomanid label gives linezolid 1200 mg; conflicts with KDT RNTCP 600 mg. Not used.
- [excluded review book, not used] states aminoglycosides are contraindicated in TB; this conflicts with KDT (second-line injectables). Not used.
- [excluded review book, not used] says isoniazid inhibits all CYP enzymes except CYP2E1; broader than KDT and GG14 (CYP2C19, CYP3A4). Not used.
- Sarkar p446-450 OCR is truncated at the INH-neuropathy sentence; the card is worded conservatively.
- [excluded review book, not used] TB drug table is garbled by OCR; not used.
