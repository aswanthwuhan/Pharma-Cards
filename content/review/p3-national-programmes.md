Conflicts and dropped items:
- Katzung16 appendix 2 gives the NTEP drug-sensitive intensive phase as four drugs "for 4 months". KDT8 ch56 (Table 56.1) gives 2 months (2HRZE/4HRE). KDT8 was used; the Katzung figure was not carded.
- NACP launch year: KDT8 ch60 says 1992, Katzung16 appendix 2 says 1987. Both are standard textbooks and disagree, so no card gives a launch year.
- Katzung16 appendix 2 says filariasis affects "more than 600 million" people in 16 states; the figure is implausible as worded (probably population at risk) and was dropped.
- The kala-azar regimen box in KDT8 ch62 has garbled footnote symbols; NVBDCP first- and second-line choices were taken from the running text (miltefosine first line, amphotericin B second line).
- Katzung16 appendix 2 says primaquine for 14 days is given "for all cases of malaria"; the card follows KDT8 ch61 (14-day primaquine for vivax, single dose for falciparum).

Overlap note: p3-tb-leprosy holds the drug-level TB and leprosy regimen cards (RNTCP 2016 regimens, MDR principles, NLEP classification and MDT doses); p3-antiparasitic holds malaria and kala-azar regimen detail; p3-antifungal-viral holds ART drugs, PEP and PMTCT. This deck keeps to programme structure, policy and programme-level drug choices.
