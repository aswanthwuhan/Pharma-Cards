# Rapid Fire review: p3-amr-general

Deck: content/rapid/p3-amr-general.json. Sources: KDT8 ch50 (Antimicrobial drugs: general considerations), KDT8 ch51 (Sulfonamides, cotrimoxazole, quinolones), KDT8 ch52 (Beta-lactam antibiotics). Review book used: RR5 (Pharma Rapid Revision 5e OCR, pp 1-5 and 21-35) for two cards only. Sarkar and Prep OCR were searched by keyword for antimicrobial content; no points were added from them.

Cards: 510 (67 class). Ids rf-p3-amr-general-0001 to 0510. Deleted before writing: 3 (listed below).

## Corrections and omissions
- Deleted: "Penicillin and cephalosporin toxicity: dose-related only". Over-general. KDT8 ch50 says hypersensitivity is unrelated to dose. Card removed.
- Deleted: two fluoroquinolone pharmacokinetic cards (combined bioavailability and half-life lines). KDT8 ch51 Table 51.1 is garbled in text extraction, so the column mapping could not be confirmed. The ciprofloxacin oral bioavailability (60-80%) card is kept as the one readable row.
- Book conflict, typhoid ceftriaxone dose: KDT8 ch51 prints 2 g IV twice daily for 2 days, then 2 g/day. KDT8 ch52 prints 4 g IV daily for 2 days, then 2 g/day. The card uses the ch51 figure. The ch52 figure is not carried. Both are the owner's textbook, so this needs a human check.
- Book conflict, C. difficile colitis: KDT8 ch50 gives metronidazole and vancomycin as drugs of choice. RR5 pp 21-35 names oral fidaxomicin first, with oral vancomycin if unavailable. The deck follows KDT8. RR5 is not added because the textbook wins on conflict.
- Layout uncertainty, KDT8 ch50 surgical prophylaxis box: the gut and biliary surgery regimen (gentamicin plus metronidazole, or amoxicillin plus clavulanate, 5 days) is read from a two-column box and may be misaligned. Check against the printed page if it matters.
- Layout uncertainty, KDT8 ch52 penicillinase-resistant list: the book names the drugs in running text, so the list is taken from the text, not from a table.
- Web cross-check: none run. No statement looked wrong enough to need one.
