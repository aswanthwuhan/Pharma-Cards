## Main corrections
- 046 (flagged): SGLT2 inhibitors rewritten from GG14 ch33 and Katzung16 ch13: mortality and HF hospitalisation benefit with or without diabetes, dapagliflozin first approved for HF (2020), empagliflozin also in HFpEF, first-line in HFpEF (Katzung), likely diuretic/preload mechanism. Card now lists the four prognostic classes (ARNI or ACEi/ARB, beta blocker, MRA, SGLT2i); the phrase "four pillars" itself is not printed in the books, so it is not used.
- 045, 052, 054: SGLT2 inhibitors and sacubitril-valsartan (recommended to replace ACEi/ARB, GG14 ch33) added to the mortality-reducing drugs; HFpEF section updated.
- 009: dual RAS blockade now "not supported" (ONTARGET, GG14 ch30) rather than "not established"; aliskiren with ACEi/ARB in diabetics avoided.
- 044: ivabradine (SHIFT) reduced HF hospitalisation and HF deaths but not total or CV mortality (GG14 ch33); card had said CV death.
- 048: digoxin range: KDT 0.5 to 1.4 ng/mL vs GG14 0.5 to 0.8 ng/mL in HF (above 1.2 linked to higher mortality).
- 049: digoxin removed from "basis of acute management"; GG14: diuretics and vasodilators, inotropes only in selected patients.
- 051: beta blockers also indicated in asymptomatic LV dysfunction after MI (card had said no benefit); not started in acute decompensation (GG14 ch33).
- 016: angiotensin II infusion is FDA-approved for vasodilatory shock (Katzung16 ch17); card had said "not used clinically".
- 027: nitroprusside cyanide/thiocyanate handling and antidotes (Katzung16 ch11).
