## Main corrections
- 012: primaquine radical cure 15 mg/day (KDT, Indian programme) vs 30 mg base/day (GG14, US CDC), both for 14 days.
- 014: WHO single low-dose primaquine 0.25 mg/kg without G6PD testing (GG14 ch66) vs 0.75 mg/kg in KDT.
- 015: tafenoquine now FDA-approved for prophylaxis and single 300 mg dose radical cure; contraindications (GG14 ch66).
- 024: parenteral artesunate replaced quinine, FDA-approved 2020 (GG14); weight-band dosing kept from KDT (3 mg/kg under 20 kg).
- 039: miltefosine contraception 3 months (KDT) vs 5 months (GG14, US guidance).
- 050: DEC contraindicated in onchocerciasis; ivermectin encephalopathy with heavy Loa loa microfilaraemia (GG14 ch68).
- 028: metronidazole no longer preferred for C. difficile (GG14 ch57). 045: triple IDA therapy for lymphatic filariasis (GG14 ch68). 056: albendazole around hydatid surgery (GG14 ch68).
- Added 057 moxidectin (GG14 ch68).

## Still needs a human check
- Visceral leishmaniasis: KDT and GG14 both describe miltefosine as first line in India; the switch to single-dose liposomal amphotericin B is not printed in the books, so cards 038/040/042 keep the book position.
- Severe malaria artesunate: GG14 defers to CDC guidelines without doses; KDT dosing kept.
