## Main corrections
- 006: fomepizole availability stated as KDT8 prints it (not commercially available in India); the "verify" note removed.
- 027: methadone rewritten: KDT detox ratio (1 mg : 4 mg morphine, 2 mg heroin, 20 mg pethidine; substitute 2 to 3 days, then taper), KDT maintenance 40 to 80 mg vs GG14 start 20 mg titrated to 60 to 80 mg; QT prolongation (GG14) and NMDA block (Katzung) added.
- 037: detox step corrected (card wrongly said "withdraw methadone over 2 to 3 days"; KDT says substitute for 2 to 3 days, then taper); clonidine/lofexidine, buprenorphine-naloxone and depot naltrexone added from GG14 ch28.
- 008, 010: "owner to verify" doses confirmed in KDT8 ch28; GG14 ch27 cautions (cardiovascular contraindication, CYP inhibition) and second-line drugs (topiramate, gabapentin, baclofen, nalmefene) added.
- 022, 023, 033: naloxone is no longer "IV only": IM, SC and 4 mg nasal spray (GG14 ch23); naloxegol and naldemedine added.
- 025, 028: CYP2D6 points (tramadol active O-demethyl metabolite; codeine ultra-rapid metabolisers, breastfed infant harm) from GG14 ch23 / Katzung16 ch31.
- 048: methylphenidate mechanism corrected to NET/DAT inhibition (RangDale9 ch49); KDT "releaser" wording noted.
- Dextropropoxyphene status (058, new): withdrawn UK/Europe/USA, banned in India 2013 (KDT8 ch34).
- Removed as ungroundable: lecanemab/donanemab, tramadol NDPS status, non-linear methadone conversion, caffeine as drug of choice for apnoea of prematurity, lorazepam-in-liver-disease. Card 060 therefore covers anti-amyloid therapy only as GG14 prints it (aducanumab).
