## Main corrections and additions (GG14 newer than KDT8)
- Streptomycin and other injectables: dropped from TB regimens (GG14 ch65). Amikacin: WHO Group C (GG14 ch65).
- Linezolid: WHO Group A for MDR-TB; part of 6-month bedaquiline-pretomanid-linezolid regimen for XDR-TB (GG14 ch65).
- C. difficile: oral vancomycin or fidaxomicin preferred; metronidazole for non-severe disease (GG14 ch57, ch60). Clindamycin: oral option for skin infection where MRSA possible (GG14 ch60).
- Vancomycin: AUC 400-600 mg.h/L target (GG14 ch58); antihistamine premedication for red man (GG14 ch58); some penicillins add renal risk (GG14 ch58). VRE: D-Ala-D-lactate target (GG14 ch58).
- Polymyxins: systemic i.v. use now for resistant serious infection, usually combined (GG14 ch59); resistance emerges in XDR Acinetobacter and Klebsiella (GG14 ch59); Stenotrophomonas and Burkholderia usually resistant (GG14 ch59).
- Nitrofurantoin macrocrystalline dose 100 mg q12h for 7 days (GG14 ch59).
- Added from GG14 only: lipoglycopeptides telavancin, dalbavancin, oritavancin (GG14 ch58); eravacycline, omadacycline (GG14 ch60).
