## Notation and reading decisions (not corrections)

- **Greek letters are dropped in the extracted text.** Receptor labels were read from context (for example "1" after "receptor" means alpha-1 or beta-1, as the surrounding text makes clear). Where a label could not be resolved, no card was written.
- **Heteroreceptors that enhance NA release** (card 0019): the printed text reads "(2 adrenergic, angiotensin AT1 and nicotinic receptors)". The Greek letter is missing. It is read as beta-2, because the same passage lists alpha-2 receptors as inhibitory, and the card says beta-2.
- **Renin release** (card 0194): KDT8 ch10 propranolol section states "decreased renin release from kidney (beta-1 mediated)". Card reads beta-1 from that line.
- **Alfuzosin** (card 0259): KDT8 ch10 prints "CYP34A". Read as CYP3A (typo). Card says CYP3A inhibitors and gives the drugs the book lists (erythromycin, ketoconazole, ritonavir).
- **Adrenaline receptor entries in Table 9.5** where the receptor subtype is printed only as "(1)" (salivary secretion, ADH secretion) were not carded, because the subtype cannot be read reliably.
- **Dobutamine "weak agonist" receptor** (KDT8 ch9) is garbled in the text. Not carded.
- **Table 9.2 and 10.2 numeric cells** where the column alignment is garbled (for example half-lives of timolol and pindolol) were not carded. Only half-lives stated in running text were used.
- **Dates stated in the book** (sibutramine banned in India from March 2011; fenfluramine withdrawal; restriction of pseudoephedrine marketing) are kept as printed. These may be dated.
