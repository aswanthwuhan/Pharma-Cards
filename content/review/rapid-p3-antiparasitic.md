## Corrected cases (GG14 newer than KDT, GG14 refs used)

- rf-p3-antiparasitic-0116: Primaquine radical-cure dose in GG14 (CDC). Answer: 30 mg base (0.5 mg/kg) daily for 14 days. Refs: GG14 ch66
- rf-p3-antiparasitic-0117: Primaquine radical-cure dose: KDT vs GG14. Answer: KDT 15 mg daily; GG14 (CDC) 30 mg base daily, twice the KDT dose. Refs: KDT8 ch61, GG14 ch66
- rf-p3-antiparasitic-0129: Single gametocidal primaquine dose: India vs WHO. Answer: India 0.75 mg/kg (45 mg); WHO 0.25 mg/kg (15 mg). Refs: KDT8 ch61, GG14 ch66
- rf-p3-antiparasitic-0130: Who gets single-dose primaquine per WHO (GG14). Answer: Non-pregnant people over 6 months, in low-transmission areas, with ACT. Refs: GG14 ch66
- rf-p3-antiparasitic-0131: G6PD testing for the single gametocidal primaquine dose (GG14). Answer: Not required at the single 0.25 mg/kg dose. Refs: GG14 ch66
- rf-p3-antiparasitic-0134: Regulatory status of tafenoquine: KDT vs GG14. Answer: KDT: awaiting approval (US-FDA filing 2017); GG14: FDA-approved for prophylaxis and radical cure. Refs: KDT8 ch61, GG14 ch66
- rf-p3-antiparasitic-0135: Tafenoquine radical-cure dose in GG14 (CDC). Answer: Single 300 mg dose with a blood schizontocide, day 1 or 2. Refs: GG14 ch66
- rf-p3-antiparasitic-0136: Tafenoquine radical-cure dose: KDT trials vs GG14 approved. Answer: KDT trials: 800 mg over 1-3 days; GG14: single 300 mg dose. Refs: KDT8 ch61, GG14 ch66
- rf-p3-antiparasitic-0140: Tafenoquine contraindications in GG14. Answer: G6PD deficiency or unknown status, pregnancy, under 16 years, primaquine hypersensitivity. Refs: GG14 ch66
- rf-p3-antiparasitic-0193: Piperaquine half-life: KDT vs GG14. Answer: KDT: 3-4 weeks; GG14: 5 weeks, longest of ACT partner drugs. Refs: KDT8 ch61, GG14 ch66
- rf-p3-antiparasitic-0198: Pyronaridine terminal half-life: KDT vs GG14. Answer: KDT: about 7 days; GG14: 14-18 days in adults. Refs: KDT8 ch61, GG14 ch66
- rf-p3-antiparasitic-0239: Severe malaria drug of choice in GG14. Answer: Parenteral artesunate replaced quinine and quinidine; FDA-approved in 2020. Refs: GG14 ch66, KDT8 ch61
- rf-p3-antiparasitic-0378: Contraception period after miltefosine: KDT vs GG14. Answer: KDT: 3 months after the course; GG14 (US guidance): 5 months. Refs: KDT8 ch62, GG14 ch67
