## Notes on book statements (kept as printed, not changed)

- KDT8 ch17 says dopamine "inhibits only LH release" (card 0098). Kept as printed; not cross-checked.
- KDT8 ch19 Table 19.2 remarks are not attached to drugs in the text. The remarks were mapped to glibenclamide, glipizide, gliclazide and glimepiride by their order in the table (cards 0421 to 0424). The mapping is layout-derived, not stated in the text.
- KDT8 ch17 ACTH and TSH cards (0044 to 0054 and 0171) are filed under the gh-somatostatin and thyroxine subtopics because the brief lists only these subtopic ids. Their refs are KDT8 ch17.
- KDT8 ch19 says GLP-1 agonists and DPP-4 inhibitors have "no hypoglycaemia" in some sections; cards state the book's wording only.
