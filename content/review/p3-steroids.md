## Key corrections
- 003: dexamethasone 0.75 mg = 20 mg hydrocortisone confirmed (KDT table); GG14 relative potency 25 added.
- 006: adrenal crisis infusion per GG14 (50-100 mg 8-hourly) and dexamethasone 4 mg for unconfirmed cases (GG14) added beside KDT 8-16 mg.
- 008: antenatal steroid now per Katzung (betamethasone 12 mg x2, 18-24 h apart, before 34 weeks); dexamethasone 6 mg 12-hourly schedule NOT used because no owner book prints it.
- 009: septic shock kept to KDT; GG14 CIRCI dosing added.
- 030/031/033/070: nitrate gap. Books give 24 h after sildenafil (KDT, GG14 ch31) and "up to 3 days" after tadalafil (KDT); GG14 says longer than 24 h especially with tadalafil. The 48 h figure from the earlier flag is not printed in any owner book, so cards say "up to 3 days (KDT)". Katzung ch12 says only 6 h (outlier, not used).
- 039: letrozole superiority in PCOS added from GG14 ch48; KDT 2011 India ban kept.
- 043/071: misoprostol is 400 microgram (KDT text extracts as "400 mg", a typo); regimen is mifepristone 600 mg + misoprostol 400 microgram at 48 h, up to 49 days, the regimen printed in KDT, GG14 and Katzung. The 200 mg/800 microgram regimen is not in the books and was not used.
- 046: unsupported VTE comparison removed; card kept to KDT.
- 049: tamoxifen/AI duration from GG14 ch73.
- 052: progestin classification completed in KDT order (megestrol, hydroxyprogesterone caproate, nomegestrol, allylestrenol added).
- 064/066/069: tocolytic and PPH cards grounded in KDT ch23, KDT ch13 (carboprost 0.25 mg) and GG14 ch46/48 (oxytocin 10 units, indomethacin limits, atosiban not FDA-approved). Tranexamic acid, heat-stable carbetocin and MgSO4 neuroprotection removed (not in books).
- 072: newer prostate-cancer antiandrogens added from GG14 ch73/ch50.
