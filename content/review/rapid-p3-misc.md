# Review: p3-misc (Rapid Fire)

Grounding pass under GROUNDING-RULES.md. Sources: KDT8 ch66-70 (topic refs), GG14 ch75 and ch40 (checks and corrections), KDT8 ch44/45 (vitamin B12, folate, vitamin K, as cited by the validated study deck), Sarkar and Rapid Revision 5 OCR (added points). Validated study deck content/decks/p3-misc.json followed for topical steroid potency, rabies schedules and psoriasis/acne therapy.

Cards: 472 total (33 class). Corrected: 2. Added from GG14/OCR: 5. Deleted: 0.

## Corrections and decisions
- Acitretin dose: KDT8 ch66 prints 0.5-0.75 mg/kg/day; GG14 ch75 (Table 75-5) prints 0.5-1 mg/kg/day. Newer GG14 used (rule 4).
- Rabies, previously vaccinated bite: KDT8 ch70 gives three i.d. doses (days 0, 3, 7); GG14 ch40 and the validated study deck give two doses (days 0, 3) with no RIG. GG14 used.
- Isotretinoin dose: kept KDT8 ch66 0.5-1 mg/kg/day for 20 weeks, as in the validated study deck. Not changed, but flag: GG14 ch75 Table 75-5 prints 0.5-2 mg/kg/day (standard) and 0.4-1.6 mg/kg/day (micronised Lidose).
- Topical steroid potency: KDT8 ch66 three-tier grading (study deck card 011) used. GG14 ch75 Table 75-3 class 1 and class 7 added as separate cards.
- Rabies schedules: KDT8 ch70 Thai intradermal regimen (0.1 mL PCEV/PVRV or 0.2 mL HDCV at 2 sites on days 0, 3, 7; 1 site on days 28 and 90) and GG14 ch40 i.m. four-dose regimen (days 0, 3, 7, 14, RIG on day 0), both as in the study deck.
- Study-deck points NOT found in the chapter text, so omitted (rule 3): OPV "USA stopped in 2000" and "about 1 per million" paralysis rate; IPV for pregnant women and HIV-positive children and "OPV contraindicated in primary immunodeficiency" (KDT8 ch70 says IPV is preferred only for adults and immunocompromised people); the blank vitamin D and Vi/Ty21a notes.
- Vitamin B12, folate and vitamin K cards cite KDT8 ch44/ch45, which are outside the topic refs but cited by the validated study deck. Vitamin D (ch24) has no validated content in the deck, so no card.
- Omitted from KDT8 ch66 as unclear in the OCR/layout: desonide strength (moderate-potency row shows no %); hydrocortisone butyrate strength printed as 0.001% (garbled). Triamcinolone 0.05% row omitted as its column is ambiguous.
- Omitted: measles "immunity lasts 8 years" (not clearly printed as intended).
- Added from OCR: Sarkar p596-600 (chlorhexidine does not inactivate SARS-CoV-2); RR5 p31-35 (ivermectin the only oral scabicide); RR5 p46-50 (warfarin first-trimester effects).
