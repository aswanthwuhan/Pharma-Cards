# Review: p1-pk-elimination (Rapid Fire)

Grounded against the owner's books per GROUNDING-RULES.md. Cards written: 264; corrected: 4; deleted: 0. Source chapters: KDT8 ch3, GG14 ch2, GG14 ch5 (from syllabus refs). Review-book citations (Sarkar, Prep, RR5) are used only for the cards marked with those refs.

## Corrections and omissions

- CYP isoform count: KDT8 ch3 says more than 100 CYP isoenzymes; GG14 ch5 (newer) says 57 in humans. Card uses GG14 ch5.
- CYP3A4 share: KDT8 ch3 says nearly 50%; GG14 ch5 says over 50%. Card uses GG14 ch5.
- Renal tubular secretion: KDT8 ch3 lists furosemide among organic bases (OCT) and labels the renal transporter OATP. GG14 ch2 lists furosemide with acidic drugs and names OAT1 and OAT3. Web cross-check (AJP Renal 2008, OAT1/OAT3 papers) also finds furosemide and thiazides handled as organic anions. Furosemide moved to the OAT card, citing GG14 ch2 and KDT8 ch3.
- Thiazides: KDT8 ch3 lists them under OCT. Web confirms organic-anion handling, but no owner-book passage read stated it, so thiazides are omitted (no card).
- Uric acid: KDT8 ch3 lists it as an OAT substrate but says it is predominantly reabsorbed. Left off the OAT secretion list.
- Omitted: Prep p36-40 advocates forced acid diuresis (ammonium chloride) for weak bases. KDT8 ch3 says this is not practised because of acidosis risk. Textbook wins; no card.
- Omitted: RR5 p1-5 lists aspirin as a zero-order drug. Not in the KDT8 ch3 nonlinear list; no card.
- Not used: Sarkar p446-450 states CYP2C19 metabolises about 40% of drugs. Conflicts with KDT8 ch3 and GG14 ch5 (CYP3A4 over 50%, CYP2D6 nearly 20%).
- Not used: Prep p46-50 says a loading dose is given for long half-life or high-clearance drugs. Conflicts with KDT8 ch3 and GG14 ch2 (loading dose depends on V and F only).
- Not used: Prep p41-45 says ritonavir inhibits CYPs irreversibly. Not in the textbooks read; no card.
- Omitted: KDT8 ch3 liposome size given as 60-80 nM (unit error, and drug-delivery material belongs to another topic).
- Study deck check: content/decks/p1-pk-elimination.json cites KDT8 only, so no GG14/Katzung newer-position override was needed.
