## Checked and kept as printed

- KD ch30: "Concurrent administration of clonazepam and valproate is contraindicated because absence status may be precipitated." GG14 ch20 calls absence status with valproate plus clonazepam rare. Card kept the KD caution ("avoid together") and does not claim it is common.
- KD ch30 and ch29 statements on phenytoin, carbamazepine, valproate, barbiturate kinetics, BZD receptor pharmacology, non-BZD hypnotics and flumazenil were checked for internal consistency. No other conflict was found in the chapter text, so no other correction was made.
- Clobazam LGS approval (card 0320) is taken from GG14 ch20 because KD ch30 does not mention it.

## Omitted (not reliably readable in extracted text)

- KD ch29 Table 29.1 (BZD hypnotic doses and half-lives): numbers are scrambled in the extraction, so no per-drug hypnotic dose cards were made from it. Clinical indications were taken from the surrounding prose.
- KD ch29 flurazepam, nitrazepam and lorazepam hypnotic doses: same reason.
- KD ch30 oxcarbazepine potency ratio ("1 times less potent"): the fraction is garbled, so the card says only that it is less potent.
- KD ch30 Table 30.1 alternative/reserve columns: column alignment is garbled, so only the first-line column was used for drug choice cards.
- GABA-A and BZD receptor subunit symbols (alpha, beta, gamma) are lost in the extraction. Cards use the subunit names in words where the text makes them clear, and do not give subunit symbols that the text lost.
- Phenytoin gum hypertrophy frequency is kept as the book states (about 20%).
