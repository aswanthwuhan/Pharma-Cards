# PYQ sources

All papers are M.D. (Pharmacology) theory papers, West Bengal University of Health Sciences (WBUHS), hosted on the Nil Ratan Sircar Medical College (NRSMC) previous-question-papers page: https://nrsmc.edu.in/pages/previous-question-papers

Each paper is 100 marks, 3 hours, 10 questions of 10 marks. The text layer was extracted with `pdftotext -layout`. Question wording is from that text layer, with obvious OCR and typo artefacts corrected (for example "normones" to "hormones"). Where a printed mark was missing in the PDF (2022 Paper II, Q3 and Q4), 10 was used per the 100-mark format.

## KNRUHS (Kaloji Narayana Rao University of Health Sciences, Telangana)

Source: official KNRUHS previous-question-papers page https://www.knruhs.telangana.gov.in/previous-question-papers/ (M.D. Pharmacology, paper codes 1200-1203). Each paper is 100 marks, 3 hours, 10 questions of 10 marks. Text layer extracted with `pdftotext -layout`. Each question is printed as "Write an essay" / "Write a short essay" with 10 x 10 = 100, so all are stored as type shortnote (10 marks). Question text is as printed, with numbering removed and wrapped lines joined. No OCR fixes were needed. Printed typos kept as-is (for example "Anti- fibrinolytic", "Derma pharmacology", "P- Glycoproteins", "Materiovigilance"). The May 2025 header reads SUPPLEMENTARY; the Oct 2025 header reads REGULAR (no supplementary label); March 2026 reads SUPPLEMENTARY.
